# The Problem to Be Solved

Imagine a bank, a company, or an institution that receives hundreds of
customer calls every day.

Customers call to:

-   Request information about services and products.
-   Find out the requirements for opening an account.
-   Report a blocked bank card.
-   Request information about loans.
-   File a complaint.
-   Find out the opening hours and contact details of branches.

**The problem:** Call center agents are sometimes overwhelmed, customers
have to wait a long time, and some simple requests unnecessarily take up
agents' time.

## The Mission

Design an artificial intelligence-powered assistant that can
automatically respond to these requests, guide customers, and make the
agents' work easier.

## What Is Expected

A presentation of a functional prototype, even a simple one.

Students are not necessarily required to develop a real telephone
system. A simulation on a computer or mobile phone is sufficient.
